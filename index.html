How the benchmark works

The point forecast starts with distance divided by planned speed and adds a correction learned from 1,800 voyages. An independent set of 900 voyages sets the interval radius from the absolute errors, either across all voyages or within each distance group.

Reference and stress results use independent sets of 1,200 synthetic voyages each. Stress adds slowdown and waiting after calibration. Observed coverage is the share of actual arrival times inside the predicted windows.
